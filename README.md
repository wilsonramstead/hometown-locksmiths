## Notes
- Self-contained: `index.html` + `styles.css` + `script.js`. Works from `file://` and with JS off.
- `noindex` while it's a demo (see comment in `<head>`). Remove before going live.
- Header call CTA is the persistent contact affordance (icon-only ≤600px, flush right). **No fixed
  bottom mobile call bar** (per house rule).
- Services shown are **review-evidenced only**: residential locksmith (rekeys, lock repair/replace,
  move-in rekeys), automotive (car keys cut & programmed, key fobs — including after a computer/ECU
  swap), commercial (business rekeys, lock hardware, padlocks/master keys), and lockouts + key
  cutting (home, auto, tractor-trailer). No invented email, license, founding year, or pricing.
- **Hours:** GBP lists Mon–Fri 9 AM–5 PM, weekends closed. Presented as regular shop hours with a
  *"call for after-hours & emergency"* note (reviews describe fast/emergency response), **not** a
  24/7 claim.
- Reviews are real Google reviews, attributed by first name + last initial (Salty Z., Evelyn B.,
  Melinda C., Grit R., Ronnie P.). Rating shown (4.8 ≥ 4.4).
